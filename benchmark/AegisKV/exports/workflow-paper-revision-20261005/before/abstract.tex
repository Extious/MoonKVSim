Agent workflows accumulate large KV caches, and a worker failure forces the
replacement replica to prefill that history before generation can resume.
\system keeps a peer-DRAM checkpoint and a Top-$K$ peer-HBM copy. Replaying
captured NOSA, NSA, and MiniCPM traces on the recorded BLSC A100-PCIe
configuration, the same arrival with no failure finishes at the same time as
a restart. One GPU failure during a 24{,}576-token workflow cuts retry
latency from 3{,}960.7\,ms to 2{,}375.3\,ms, and every later workflow keeps
that 1{,}585.4\,ms gap. The saving grows with the checkpointed prefix. The
HBM blocks move completion by at most 0.217\,ms.
