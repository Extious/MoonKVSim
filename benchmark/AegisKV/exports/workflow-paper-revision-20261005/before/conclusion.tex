\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\system keeps a complete peer-DRAM checkpoint and a Top-$K$ HBM copy, placed
by Router on a peer outside the failed worker. On the recorded BLSC
A100-PCIe configuration, captured NOSA, NSA, and MiniCPM arrivals finish at
the same time as a restart when no worker fails. One GPU failure during a
24{,}576-token workflow shortens that workflow and every later one by
1{,}585.4\,ms relative to restarting with no copy. The saving is 128.0\,ms
at 2{,}048 tokens and grows with the checkpointed prefix. The HBM blocks
move completion by at most 0.217\,ms. $K{=}16$ covers only a few percent of
the distinct blocks in the trace, so the DRAM checkpoint remains the copy
that matters.
