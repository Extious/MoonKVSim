\subsection{Insights and Design Implications}
\label{sec:design-insights}

Three premises motivate the design (Figure~\ref{fig:design-insights}).
Their evidence supports different levels of confidence.

\paragraph{Insight 1: execution structure supports timing estimates.}
Recurring roles and task stages expose useful predictive inputs.
Pythia profiles roles and workflow structure~\cite{yu2026pythia};
for known model, request, and device configurations, Wang et al.
report runtime WAPE of 7.25\%, versus 11.05\% for GBDT
(Figure~\ref{fig:design-insights}(\subref{fig:insight-agent-time}))~\cite{wang2026runtimecandidate}.
This motivates estimating stage costs and updating completion times as
execution progresses. Whole-agent remaining time additionally depends on
future rounds, tools, and serving delays; its accuracy requires separate
validation. The Tracker therefore separates estimation from DAG slack
propagation and conservatively handles unknown successors.

\paragraph{Insight 2: sparse attention changes the recovery objects.}
Long-context models use sparse mechanisms including InfLLMv2,
DSA, or compressed/hybrid attention~\cite{minicpm4model,
openbmb2025minicpm41,deepseek2025v32exp,deepseek2025v32model,
zai2026glm5model,openbmb2026sala,deepseek2026v4model,minimax2026m3,
zai2026glm52,longcat2026sparse}.
Figure~\ref{fig:design-insights}(\subref{fig:insight-sparse-adoption})
establishes release examples, not deployment share.
The Planner therefore consumes actual layer/block selections and
model-specific recovery state instead of valuing all KV bytes equally.

\paragraph{Insight 3: past selections predict part of retry demand.}
At equal byte budgets, historical frequency covers 79.2\%, 77.8\%, and
53.6\% of distinct needed KV for NOSA-1B, NOSA-3B, and MiniCPM4,
versus 59.7\%, 59.7\%, and 50.9\% for recency
(Figure~\ref{fig:design-insights}(\subref{fig:insight-retry-kv})). The gain is
model-dependent and incomplete. These held-out book-text suffixes
motivate advance placement with exact demand fetches for misses;
they do not establish performance on agentic failure retries.
