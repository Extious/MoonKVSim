\section{AegisKV Design}
\label{sec:design}

AegisKV allocates scarce Peer HBM and copy bandwidth to reduce the
effect of failures on workflow completion time (WCT).
We first derive the design from workflow slack, sparse KV access, and
transfer--compute overlap. These observations separate three decisions:
which requests merit protection, which of their KV objects merit Peer
residency, and how requests resume after failure. Protection priority
does not impose a serial order on recovery execution.
