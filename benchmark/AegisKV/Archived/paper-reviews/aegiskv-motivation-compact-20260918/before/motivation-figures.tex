  \caption{Motivation for workflow-aware KV protection.
    (a) Each of 121 CC/Weka workflows contributes its largest
    branch-duration gap across strictly overlapping groups with inferred
    fan-in (median: 138\,s; P90: 1,173\,s). Inference uses a Main request
    after all branches finish; Main activity during branches is allowed.
    (b) Ten parallel branches with a 168\,s duration gap. Time zero is
    the first branch launch; widths encode durations and the right-hand
    column reports seconds. Dashed tails show slack to the 221.8\,s join,
    determined by A10. Main boxes indicate inferred dependencies only.
    (c) Ten independent branch-failure counterfactuals at 36.1\,s after
    the fork. Hatched time is absorbed by slack; solid time delays WCT.
    Costs use the Qwen3-14B static configuration in
    Figure~\ref{fig:motivation-recompute}, including an assumed 64-token
    tail. The case and fault instant illustrate greater fan-out and
    recovery exposure, not population-average failure impact.}
  \label{fig:motivation}
  \Description{The panels show workflow-maximum duration gaps, a time-scaled
    ten-branch workflow, and estimated rebuilding cost decomposed into
    slack-absorbed time and WCT loss. The workflow gap is 168 seconds,
    compared with a population median of 138 seconds. A10 determines the
    assumed join and exposes 27.5 seconds of estimated rebuilding to WCT.
    A5 costs 45.7 seconds to rebuild, but its 146.1-second slack absorbs
    this delay. Other branches also absorb their entire costs.}
