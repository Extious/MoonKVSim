\section{AegisKV Design}
\label{sec:design}

AegisKV combines workflow delay tolerance with each request's KV
recovery demand. The Tracker estimates slack; the Recovery Manager binds
takeover destinations at arrival; the KV Manager chooses backup tiers
and hot blocks; each Adapter controls its Peer capacity and resident
layer prefixes over time. The following design
separates these decisions from the evidence that motivates them.
