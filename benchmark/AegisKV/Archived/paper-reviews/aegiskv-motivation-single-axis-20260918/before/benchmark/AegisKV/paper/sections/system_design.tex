\section{AegisKV Design}
\label{sec:design}

AegisKV combines workflow delay tolerance with each request's KV
recovery demand. The Tracker estimates slack; the Planner selects
protection tiers and capacity-bounded layer/block sets; the runtime
commits replicas and executes concurrent retries. The following design
separates these decisions from the evidence that motivates them.
