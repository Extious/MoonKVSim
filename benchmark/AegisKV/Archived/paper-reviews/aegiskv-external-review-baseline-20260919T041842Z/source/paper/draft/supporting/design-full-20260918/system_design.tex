\section{AegisKV Design}
\label{sec:design}

AegisKV allocates scarce Peer HBM and copy bandwidth to reduce the
effect of failures on workflow completion time (WCT).
We organize the design around agent-time estimation, the adoption of
sparse attention in long-context serving, and prediction of the KV
needed to replay an uncommitted tail. We distinguish supporting evidence
from the timing-estimation premise that remains unvalidated.
These premises lead to workflow protection tiers and request-internal
KV selection; transfer--compute overlap and capacity constraints then
determine placement. Protection priority does not impose a serial order
on recovery execution.
