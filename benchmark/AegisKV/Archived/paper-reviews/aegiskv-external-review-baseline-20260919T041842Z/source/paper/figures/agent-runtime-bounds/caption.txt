Analytical references for runtime prediction. Colored curves are lower bounds on the runtime-weighted fraction of configurations whose relative prediction error is at most the horizontal-axis threshold, derived using Markov's inequality and the reported Runtime WAPE of Wang et al. (arXiv:2609.03335v1, Table 1): Predictor 7.25% and GBDT 11.05%. The dashed reference assumes zero prediction error. These are not measured CDFs, configuration-count fractions, or attainable-optimum results. The source studies configuration-conditioned execution costs, not complete multi-round agent durations. Calculations use the published rounded WAPE values.
