\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{A deliberate evidence-lineage mismatch.}
The system description is the current source-owned independent-tier design,
whereas the WCT artifacts were produced by a superseded prototype.  We chose
not to relabel the old results or translate their legacy L1/L2 states into
Peer HBM/Store DRAM.  Consequently, the reported WCT answers whether earlier
KV protection reduced recomputation delay, but not whether the present
admission, two-level victim indexes, independent ACKs, or HBMQuotaAIMD improve
WCT.  The separate current-design campaign verifies control direction,
occupancy, intended residency, reuse, and conditioned coverage only.  A
current-design WCT campaign must rerun the same workload and baselines from
one frozen source/config manifest before making an end-to-end claim.

\paragraph{Small samples, projection, and incomplete inference.}
The DP8 comparison has 24 paired workflows from one seed/trial.  Its paired
points are more informative than treating request records as independent, but
there are too few independent trials for a robust confidence interval.  The
DP64 result is a model projection, not an observed scale-out run.  The
heavy-failure panels contain 20 workflows each, no replication interval, and
an unsuccessful alpha/beta pressure check.  We report all three because they
answer complementary WCT questions, but we do not pool them, attach a common
confidence interval, or infer a scaling law.

\paragraph{Prediction and online control.}
Slack is a priority signal, not a correctness condition.  Unseen branches or
an incomplete visible frontier can zero or reorder priorities; capacity,
chunk validity, version checks, and output fences remain independent of
prediction quality.  SlackLandlord is a bounded-overhead online policy, not a
global optimum: variable chunks, prefix constraints, independent tiers,
joint residency, and asynchronous copies preclude Landlord's original
competitive ratio.  None of the historical WCT artifacts isolates prediction
error or the present Algorithms~1--3, so their individual contribution to WCT
is unknown.

\paragraph{Interpreting alpha.}
Algorithm~3 reacts to foreground HBM pressure and stable windows, not directly
to failure rate.  Lower load can raise alpha and sustained pressure can drive
the effective quota to zero, but more failures alone need not raise it.  The
legacy fixed-$\alpha$ DP8 comparison cannot establish adaptation benefit, and
the heavy-failure artifact explicitly fails its pressure-validity check.  The
current-design telemetry does establish the intended feedback direction:
pressure grows 0.018$\rightarrow$0.590 and mean alpha falls
0.868$\rightarrow$0.129 across the concurrency sweep, while two high-pressure
scale/model cells spend about 27\% of steady windows at alpha zero.  It still
does not quantify the controller's WCT benefit.

\paragraph{Simulator and fault envelope.}
Every quantitative result is from SGLSim.  The legacy WCT and current
mechanism campaigns are separate lineages and are not pooled.  Service curves, failure timing,
logical PP lanes, and transfer rates are controlled abstractions, not
measurements of deployed Mooncake.  The artifacts omit or simplify physical
worker replacement, correlated Store/Router failure, shared-NIC queueing,
CUDA overlap, kernel interference, and healthy-path copy contention.  The
DP64 projection additionally omits correlated outage, co-failure, and queue
spillover.  Thus, these results neither establish physical fail-stop recovery
nor quantify deployed latency, throughput, bandwidth, or availability.

\paragraph{Mechanism coverage.}
The current campaign's 92.63\% request reuse and 98.31\% prompt-token reuse are
workflow-local prefix properties; the simulator explicitly does not
deduplicate protection copies using reuse.  Low central mean occupancy
(0.856\% HBM and 0.253\% DRAM), zero HBM-only admissions, and zero DRAM
evictions across the full-policy scenario matrix mean the results do not
stress every storage-control branch.  Conditioned restored-token fractions
likewise describe fixed live-state probes, not IID failures or online
availability.

\paragraph{Baseline scope.}
The paired DP8 data provide direct no-backup, host-only, peer-GPU, and legacy
AegisKV comparisons.  They are prototype controls rather than faithful
reproductions of DejaVu, RaidServe, KevlarFlow, or GhostServe.  The fact that
host-only and peer-GPU backup beat legacy AegisKV in this trial is a material
negative result.  It motivates, rather than validates, the current design's
independent tier admission and pressure-aware quota control.
