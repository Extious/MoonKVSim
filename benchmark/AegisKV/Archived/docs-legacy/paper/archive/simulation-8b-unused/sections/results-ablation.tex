\subsection{Which Policy Components Matter?}
\label{sec:ablation-results}
\input{sections/figures/cost-ablation}

The four-arm ablation holds candidate envelopes fixed and pairs every arm
with its own failure-free run. Replacing the full retention subsystem with
LRU increases the focus failure penalty by 7.377~s on average (95\% interval
[1.953, 12.800]~s); all five seed contrasts favor full. This supports the
full retention subsystem for these selected interventions. It does not
isolate the landlord score from the safe-tail lifecycle changed by this arm,
and the interval is an exploratory, unadjusted comparison.

Disabling slack increases the mean focus penalty by 6.818~s, but its interval
is wide: [$-14.298$, 27.935]~s. Four seeds favor full and one favors disabled
slack. Fixing the quota by disabling AIMD gives a 5.511~s mean contrast with
interval [$-8.676$, 19.699]~s; three seeds favor full and two favor fixed
quota. These five repetitions do not resolve a consistent incremental benefit
for either component.

The focus result does not establish a whole-workload advantage. The
ablation-minus-full mean failure-penalty contrasts for disabled slack, LRU,
and disabled AIMD are $-0.073$, $-0.111$, and $-0.132$~s, respectively; all
three intervals cross zero. Mean F1 protection traffic is 4,724~GiB for full,
4,952~GiB without slack, 6,192~GiB with LRU, and 4,248~GiB without AIMD.
Full and disabled AIMD recover the same selected prefix fraction in every
seed despite their mixed completion effects. Together, these observations
distinguish retention efficiency and focus completion from aggregate benefit;
they do not establish dominance of all three components across workloads.

Figure~\ref{fig:cost-ablation}(a,b) relates the seven sensitivity settings to
their protection cost and recovered fraction. Each point changes concurrency
or failure exposure as well as resource use, so these associations do not
isolate a cost-effect tradeoff. Panel~(d) preserves the five lowest exact
focus-workflow gains. The worst case is seed~4, F4/C16: Store-only completes
the workflow in 14.08~s and AegisKV in 43.38~s. Such regressions remain visible
even when a setting's mean or within-focus maximum favors AegisKV.
