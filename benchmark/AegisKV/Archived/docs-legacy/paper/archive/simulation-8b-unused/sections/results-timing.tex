\subsection{Dependence on Service Composition}
\label{sec:timing-results}
\input{sections/figures/timing}

Figure~\ref{fig:timing} repeats the comparison with fixed logical focus
requests under maximum-share, intermediate and summed-share step durations.
For $\beta=0,0.5,1$, the mean normalized focus failure-penalty differences
are 4.25\%, $-6.76\%$ and 14.08\%; their respective intervals are
[$-77.19\%$, 85.69\%], [$-47.12\%$, 33.59\%] and
[$-25.91\%$, 54.07\%]. Whole-workload mean penalty differences are 0.090,
0.032 and $-0.039$~s, also with intervals crossing zero. The estimated
direction changes with service composition, and none resolves a policy
advantage. This supports retaining the model dependence in the conclusion;
it establishes neither equivalence nor a calibrated hardware bound.
The focus requests are fixed across these models but differ from the primary
compatibility-conditioned selection, so $\beta=0$ is not another estimate
of the primary focus effect.
