\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{KV storage and fault tolerance.}
Mooncake coordinates distributed KV storage~\cite{qin2025mooncake};
D\'ej\`aVu streams KV for disaggregation and recovery~\cite{strati2024dejavu}.
GhostServe uses coded protection~\cite{jayakody2026ghostserve}, and RaidServe
addresses post-failure compute/memory imbalance~\cite{xu2026raidserve}.
AegisKV values request-owned protection by workflow Slack, with independent
tiers and no parity. These systems are not measured baselines; DRAM-only
is an AegisKV control.

\paragraph{Workflow-aware serving.}
Parrot exposes application dataflow~\cite{lin2024parrot}; SGLang combines
structured execution and radix reuse~\cite{zheng2024sglang}. KVFlow uses
step graphs and temporal proximity for caching~\cite{pan2025kvflow},
Pythia exploits predictability~\cite{yu2026pythia}, and Cortex isolates
stage pools~\cite{pagonas2025cortex}. AegisKV estimates fail-now recovery
harm using predicted delay tolerance. Its salted prototype provides no
cross-request prefix reuse.

\paragraph{Caching and control.}
Strata couples cache-transfer layout and scheduling~\cite{xie2026strata};
our model reproduces neither its kernels nor throughput. AegisKV combines
CPM float~\cite{kelley1959cpm}, Landlord credit~\cite{young2002landlord} and
AIMD feedback~\cite{chiu1989aimd} with prefix dependencies and asynchronous
commitment. Their classical guarantees do not automatically extend to this
coupled problem.
