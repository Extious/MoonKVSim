\section{Physical Prototype Validation}
\label{sec:physical}
The prototype runs Qwen3-8B FP16 on two RTX4090 GPUs (DP2, TP=PP=1).
Absent CUDA peer access, transfers use host-staged TCP with D2H/H2D.
Each arm executes six synthetic two-branch workflows (12 requests), with
one worker process-group termination 1.5 s after activation, independent
of backup commitment.

All requests complete. Full records six HBM-only admissions and seven
bypasses, protects 598.5 MiB, and restores 1,264/1,278 prompt tokens before
completing 512 output tokens. DRAM naturally admits nothing. Directed
tests separately exercise both tiers (58.5 MiB each), excluded from
Table~\ref{tab:physical}. Full batch completion is 0.46\% slower than
unprotected. One trial per arm supports functional recovery and cleanup;
non-streaming completion does not validate live output fencing.

Same-allocation calibration yields HBM/DRAM write rates of 0.587/0.336 GB/s,
load rates of 2.300/1.233 GB/s and recomputation cost 0.1007 ms/token.
These include software overhead and do not calibrate A100, P2P or RDMA.
The predictor uses 80 synthetic workflows (40/20/20 train/calibration/test),
with held-out duration error 2.68\% and Slack coverage 95\%; long-context
and dynamic-agent generalization remain unvalidated.

The pilot registers 2 GiB HBM, initial $\alpha=0.5$ and a 5 s AIMD interval;
simulation uses 40 GiB, 0.2 and 1 s. Equal quota formulas do not imply equal
operating points. Stable windows, additive updates, quota publication and
cleanup have physical evidence; pressure-driven tail reclamation does not.
