\section{Conclusion}
AegisKV connects workflow Slack with independent HBM/DRAM protection,
suffix-safe admission and pressure-controlled quotas. The Mooncake prototype
validates functional recovery; the aligned simulation explicitly accounts for
copying, commit visibility, ownership and cleanup. Full incurs 91.8\%
more healthy protection traffic than DRAM-only without a resolved workflow
latency advantage. Other branches and queues can outweigh faster recovery.
Calibrated timing and long-context prediction remain necessary to identify
an operating regime with reliable end-to-end gains.
