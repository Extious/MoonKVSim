\section{Limitations and Validity}
Prescribed durations/output counts and static topology do not validate dynamic
agents, answer quality or long-context prediction. Ideal Slack, prompt-only
protection, coarser allocation and uncalibrated timing limit extrapolation.
The pilot tests one process failure per arm, excluding pressure races,
streaming, TP/PP recovery, control-plane failure and partitions.

Five admission orders on one finite workload, policy-dependent collateral
victims and few fault positions limit external validity. Intervals are
exploratory without multiplicity correction. Earlier replacement gaps, ordinal
sealing and request salts limit redundancy/reuse; faster recovery need not
improve workflows.

Revision 3 has no native rollout or physical 1\% certificate. Staging reduces
steady capacity; greedy bounded groups may miss better exchanges; snapshot
invalidation and single-group concurrency can delay protection. Shared state
must be immutable and prefix-sufficient. Memory safety assumes real reservations
and bounded cancellation. Old results do not evaluate the revision.
