\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Fault-tolerant LLM serving.}
D\'ej\`aVu develops efficient KV streaming and state replication
\cite{strati2024dejavu}. GhostServe studies lightweight checkpointing
\cite{jayakody2026ghostserve}. LUMEN coordinates checkpointing and recovery
with serving load \cite{cao2026lumen}. These are more than isolated copy
mechanisms; in particular, load-aware recovery is already addressed by
LUMEN. \system's distinction is its use of Agent-task delay tolerance and
request-internal demand to allocate finite protection resources. External
system reproductions and inspired internal baselines are identified
separately in the evaluation protocol.

\paragraph{Workflow-aware execution and live cache reuse.}
Parrot exposes application dependencies through semantic variables
\cite{lin2024parrot}. KVFlow uses workflow proximity for prefix eviction
and prefetching \cite{pan2025kvflow}; TOPAS jointly considers prefix states
and task progress \cite{ni2026topas}. These normal-serving objectives are
complementary to recovery. PagedAttention/vLLM, SGLang, and CachedAttention
manage or reuse live KV state
\cite{kwon2023vllm,zheng2024sglang,gao2024cachedattention}. A recovery copy
additionally requires reachability after the protected domain fails and a
complete compatible committed state.

\paragraph{Native sparse attention and recovery overlap.}
Native sparse models expose selected historical KV regions
\cite{huang2026nosav2,fasa2026minicpm4nsa,openbmb2025minicpm41}.
\system consumes those indices without modifying the attention choice.
The relevant placement evidence is causal future-demand coverage and
exposed waiting removed beyond a full DRAM checkpoint, rather than
retrospective concentration alone. Layer-ordering and transfer/compute
overlap are also used in normal cache systems
\cite{gao2024cachedattention,pan2025kvflow}; the evaluation isolates their
value during recovery.

\paragraph{Quota control and applications.}
The Adapter uses guarded additive increase and multiplicative decrease,
following the general congestion-control principle \cite{chiu1989aimd}.
The experiments test its total quota against fixed-budget controls and
foreground demand. DeepResearchBench, DeepResearch Bench II, and
SWE-bench Verified provide public task inputs
\cite{du2025deepresearch,li2026deepresearchii,swebenchverified};
their task/report datasets do not themselves provide the complete
multi-Agent serving traces required by this paper.
