\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

\system allocates complete Peer-DRAM protection by workflow-exposed
reconstruction delay, places selected early-layer KV in Peer-HBM, and
adapts its quota to foreground capacity. Two public-task-based applications
make Agent-task dependencies, parallel readiness, and consecutive local
calls explicit. The evaluation design measures recovery benefit together
with healthy cost, common-failure effects, prediction error, and the
incremental contribution of HBM. Its final quantitative conclusions will
be based on the new application traces and paired physical campaign.
